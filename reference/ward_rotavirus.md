# Ward rotavirus human-challenge dose-response data

Grouped binomial infection counts from the Ward et al. rotavirus
human-challenge study, in which healthy adult volunteers ingested
measured doses of live CJN-strain rotavirus and were monitored for
infection. Each row is one dose group, in the raw 3-column shape
accepted by
[`as_dose_response()`](https://seanthimons.github.io/singlehit/reference/as_dose_response.md):
the administered `dose` and the numbers of subjects who did (`positive`)
and did not (`negative`) respond. Pass it straight to
[`as_dose_response()`](https://seanthimons.github.io/singlehit/reference/as_dose_response.md),
[`analyze_dose_response()`](https://seanthimons.github.io/singlehit/reference/analyze_dose_response.md),
or
[`read_dose_response()`](https://seanthimons.github.io/singlehit/reference/read_dose_response.md)
(via the bundled file, see source) as the canonical example dataset for
the package.

## Usage

``` r
ward_rotavirus
```

## Format

A [tibble](https://tibble.tidyverse.org/reference/tibble.html) with 8
rows and 3 columns:

- dose:

  Administered rotavirus dose in focus-forming units (FFU), spanning
  roughly seven orders of magnitude (0.009 to 90000).

- positive:

  Number of subjects in the dose group who became infected.

- negative:

  Number of subjects in the dose group who did not.

## Source

Ward RL, Bernstein DI, Young EC, Sherwood JR, Knowlton DR, Schiff GM
(1986). "Human rotavirus studies in volunteers: determination of
infectious dose and serological response to infection." *Journal of
Infectious Diseases*, 154(5), 871-880. Distributed with the package at
`inst/extdata/Ward_rotavirus.txt`; regenerated by
`data-raw/ward_rotavirus.R`.
